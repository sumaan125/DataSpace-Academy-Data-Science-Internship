# Data Analysis of Iris, IPL and Life Expectancy Datasets (Statistical Tools)

**Notebook:** [data_analysis_IRIS_flower,IPL_and_Life_Expectancy_datasets.ipynb](data_analysis_IRIS_flower,IPL_and_Life_Expectancy_datasets.ipynb)
**Data:** `iris.csv` (150 flowers), `deliveries (2).csv` (IPL ball-by-ball, about 150k rows), `Life Expectancy Data.csv` (country-wise health indicators, about 2.9k rows)

## Statistics covered
- **Central tendency:** mean (and the effect of outliers), median, mode, which to use
- **Probability:** PMF, PDF, CDF, binomial distribution, why histograms lead to PDFs
- **Spread:** range, percentiles, quartiles, interquartile range, mean absolute deviation, variance, standard deviation
